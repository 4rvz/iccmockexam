Kowloon Base • Hong Kong

14-Day Chill Trip

October 4–17, 2026 — malling, sightseeing, local neighborhoods, harbour evenings, food, island trips and real rest days.

Your travel style: slow and comfortable. One main area per day, mostly late starts, simple MTR routes and four proper rest days.
🏨 Kowloon🚇 MTR-first🛍️ Shopping🌃 Skyline🍜 Local food😴 Rest days

Day-by-day plan

OCT 4 • ARRIVAL

Check-in + Victoria Harbour evening

Very easy

Check in, unpack, load an Octopus card and have dinner. If you still have energy, walk along the Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront.

How to get there: MTR → Tsim Sha Tsui or East Tsim Sha Tsui → walk toward Avenue of Stars.

HK$150–300

OCT 5

Tsim Sha Tsui + K11 + Harbour City

Easy

Start 11 AM–12 PM. Explore K11 MUSEA, Avenue of Stars and Harbour City. Stay around the waterfront into the evening.

Route: Tsim Sha Tsui → K11 MUSEA → Avenue of Stars → Harbour City → waterfront.

HK$200–400 excluding shopping

😴
OCT 6

Rest day

Sleep, laundry, café, nearby food. No sightseeing required.

OCT 7

Mong Kok + Ladies' Market + Temple Street

Moderate

Start around 2 PM. Enjoy Kowloon's dense street atmosphere, markets, shops and local food.

Route: Mong Kok MTR → Langham Place → Sneakers Street → Ladies' Market → Yau Ma Tei → Temple Street at night.

HK$200–500 excluding shopping

☕
OCT 8

Rest day / Elements Mall

Stay in or have a comfortable indoor afternoon at Elements above Kowloon Station.

OCT 9

Central + SoHo + Tai Kwun + The Peak

Big day

Take the Star Ferry from Tsim Sha Tsui to Central. Explore Central slowly before heading up to Victoria Peak near sunset.

Route: Star Ferry → Central → Mid-Levels Escalator → PMQ → Tai Kwun → SoHo → Peak Tram.

HK$350–600

OCT 10

Nan Lian Garden + Chi Lin + Wong Tai Sin

Easy

A peaceful day of traditional architecture, gardens and temples followed by an easy mall dinner.

Route: Diamond Hill MTR → Nan Lian Garden → Chi Lin Nunnery → Wong Tai Sin → Plaza Hollywood.

HK$150–300

🛌
OCT 11

Rest day

Recharge before Lantau. Massage, coffee, hotel time or dinner nearby.

OCT 12

Ngong Ping + Big Buddha + Tai O

Big day

Your main Lantau excursion: cable car, monastery, Big Buddha and fishing-village atmosphere.

Route: MTR → Tung Chung → Ngong Ping 360 → Po Lin Monastery / Tian Tan Buddha → bus to Tai O.

HK$500–800

OCT 13

M+ + West Kowloon Cultural District

Easy

Explore M+, then spend late afternoon outdoors at Art Park and the waterfront. Dinner at Elements afterward.

Route: Kowloon / Austin area → M+ → Art Park → waterfront → Elements.

HK$300–500

🌿
OCT 14

Rest day

Sleep late, have a slow lunch and return only to places you genuinely want to revisit.

OCT 15

Cheung Chau Island

Moderate

Enjoy Hong Kong at island pace: ferry ride, seafood, snacks, waterfront walks and Tung Wan Beach.

Route: MTR → Central/Hong Kong Station → Central Pier 5 → ferry to Cheung Chau.

HK$250–450

OCT 16

Causeway Bay + Ding Ding + final shopping

Easy

Spend your last full day malling and enjoying Hong Kong Island from the top deck of a classic tram.

Route: Causeway Bay MTR → Times Square → Hysan Place → Victoria Park → tram toward Wan Chai/Central.

HK$250–500 excluding shopping

OCT 17 • DEPARTURE

Breakfast + airport

Very easy

Breakfast near the hotel, check out and leave plenty of time for Hong Kong International Airport.

Simple option: Kowloon Station → Airport Express → airport.
